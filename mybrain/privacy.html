mybrain privacy policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

mybrain is a private tool used only by its owner and his household. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

With the account holder's consent, mybrain accesses Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive and Google Tasks data of the Google accounts its owner has connected.

How the data is used

The data is used only to show, search and organize the account holder's own information, and to create drafts, events, tasks and documents when the account holder asks for them.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's personal computer. Google user data is not sold, not shared with third parties, and not used for advertising. mybrain's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
